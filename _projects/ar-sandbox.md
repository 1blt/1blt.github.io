An augmented reality sandbox that bridges video gamers and tactile play through real-time depth sensing and projection mapping.

- **Stack:** Unity, KinectSDK, Microsoft Kinect V2, Pico Projector
- **Hardware:** EM River 4 sandbox
- **Idea:** Levels the playing field—childhood tactile skills vs. gaming expertise
