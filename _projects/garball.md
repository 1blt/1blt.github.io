A gumball machine for trash that transforms recycling into an interactive experience about waste and consumption.

- **Materials:** PVC, nylon, netting
- **Featured:** Edward Humes, Pulitzer Prize-winning journalist
- **Idea:** Making the invisible waste stream visible through playful interaction
